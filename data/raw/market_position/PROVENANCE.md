## Why this approach

Milestone 1 identified Momentum Works as the best candidate for Indonesia platform competitive position. Full report matrices were not freely available. This curated file captures only figures that were publicly reproduced with identifiable citations.

## Rules applied

- Annual grain only (no monthly interpolation)
- Empty cells left empty when GMV or rank not stated
- Combined Tokopedia + TikTok Shop rows flagged via `entity_type=combined`
- Estimates labeled; not treated as official company GMV
- Years without a verified public Indonesia platform citation (notably 2021) omitted rather than fabricated
