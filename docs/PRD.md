# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview

**E-Commerce Power Shift: Shopee vs Tokopedia in Indonesia** is a competitive-intelligence and data-analytics investigation into the changing competitive position of Shopee and Tokopedia in Indonesia.

The project uses publicly available and traceable data to describe how relative competitive position evolved, which observable market indicators moved alongside that evolution, and what the current trajectory may imply under reasonable future scenarios.

Important research principle: do **not** assume that Shopee's rise (or any relative shift) was caused by any specific factor. Potential explanations are hypotheses to be investigated later. Evidence may support, weaken, or fail to speak to each hypothesis.

## Primary Research Question

How did Shopee's competitive position in Indonesia change relative to Tokopedia, and what observable market factors help explain that shift?

## Secondary Research Questions

- When did the competitive gap between Shopee and Tokopedia materially change?
- How did their relative market position evolve over time?
- How did consumer search interest evolve?
- What observable indicators moved alongside the competitive shift?
- What major competitive, strategic, regulatory, or market events occurred around important turning points?
- What evidence supports or contradicts possible explanations?
- What might the current trajectory imply under reasonable future scenarios?

## Business Problem

This is a competitive-intelligence problem.

A strategy or commercial team wants to understand how the Indonesian e-commerce landscape changed, what measurable factors are associated with that change, and what the current trajectory may imply.

The deliverable is not a predetermined narrative about why one platform “won.” It is an evidence-backed account of what changed, what moved with that change, what remains uncertain, and what scenarios are plausible given the available data.

## Non-Goals

- No proprietary internal Shopee data
- No proprietary internal Tokopedia data
- No invented internal revenue
- No invented user counts
- No invented operational metrics
- No unsupported causal claims
- No fabricated sources, market-share values, historical values, or findings
- No Claude-based workflow, agents, or tooling

## Success Criteria

The final project should have:

- Traceable data sources
- Reproducible analysis
- Documented metric definitions
- Clear separation between fact and inference
- Validated analytical outputs
- Transparent forecasting assumptions
- Dashboard metrics traceable to source data
