## Direct answers

### A. At the end of 2025, did Tokopedia make a comeback?

**UNSUPPORTED**

**Why:**

- Standalone Legacy Tokopedia 2025 GMV and share are **UNKNOWN / MISSING**.  
- The only published 2025 MW-style figure for the Tokopedia side is **Combined** Tokopedia + TikTok Shop (~38% / ~US$21.9B) — **NOT COMPARABLE** to Legacy Tokopedia 2024 (23%).  
- APJII access for Tokopedia was essentially flat (9.40% → 9.57%) while Shopee and TTS surged — OBSERVED access, not GMV.  
- Momentum Works publicly linked Indonesia’s slow GMV growth partly to **Tokopedia GMV rationalisation** after the TikTok acquisition — PRIMARY qualitative against a Legacy boom narrative.

**What we do *not* conclude:** That Legacy Tokopedia GMV necessarily collapsed by a specific amount. That would require the forbidden residual (Combined − TTS) or a fabricated fill.

### B. Was the 2025 growth primarily TikTok-led?

**PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**

**Why:**

- TTS Indonesia GMV ~US$13.1B (2025), entity-labeled TikTok Shop — OBSERVED (SECONDARY_HIGH).  
- APJII TTS access 12.20% → 27.37% — OBSERVED attention shift toward TTS.  
- TikTok holds controlling ownership; post-regulation design routes TikTok-app commerce through the enlarged Tokopedia entity — OBSERVED structure.  
- MW rationalisation language is consistent with Legacy cleanup while TTS-labeled commerce scaled — OBSERVED qualitative + OBSERVED TTS scale.

**Why only partial:** Combined Indonesia ~US$21.9B still includes Tokopedia by definition; the public record does **not** prove what share of that Combined GMV is Legacy vs TTS. Attribution of the **entire** Combined figure to TikTok alone would overclaim.

   **No — UNSUPPORTED** on available evidence.

4. **Primarily TikTok-led with Tokopedia as infrastructure/partner?**  
   **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** — strongest available reading; Combined GMV split still incomplete.

5. **By end-2025, recovering standalone marketplace vs Combined-only growth?**  
   Evidence supports **growth/visibility of the Combined operation and TTS-labeled metrics**, not a verified standalone Legacy Tokopedia recovery.
