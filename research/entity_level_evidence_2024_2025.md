## Critical methodological findings

1. **Structural break is confirmed by primary sources** (GoTo PR: businesses combined under PT Tokopedia) and by MW secondary reporting (2025 Combined share).
2. **Standalone Legacy Tokopedia 2025 GMV/share remains unavailable** in free public MW reproductions.
3. **Residual math (21.9 − 13.1) is rejected as observed Legacy GMV** — sources do not state the figures are additive and non-overlapping.
4. **APJII ≠ GMV.** Some secondary articles (e.g. Digivestasi cited elsewhere) appear to recycle APJII access percentages as if they were market share — **rejected**.
5. **SEA US$45.6B is TTS platform GMV**, not automatically Combined-with-Tokopedia (Digital in Asia / MW Low Down distinction). Bisnis text that labels $45.6B as Combined regional is treated as likely conflation.
6. **GoTo fee confirms combined GMV basis** but cannot recover entity split.
