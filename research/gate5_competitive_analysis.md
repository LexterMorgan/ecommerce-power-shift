**Project:** E-Commerce Power Shift: Shopee vs Tokopedia in Indonesia  
**Question:** Who actually drove the 2025 Indonesian e-commerce power shift?  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Evidence base:** Gate 3B entity-split + Gate 4 exploratory synthesis only  
**No SQL / dashboard.** No fabricated Legacy Tokopedia 2025 GMV/share.

**Entity vocabulary (do not collapse):** Shopee · Legacy Tokopedia · TikTok Shop Indonesia (TTS) · Combined Tokopedia+TTS · Indonesia total

---

# 1. Executive conclusion

**Who drove the 2025 shift?**

**OBSERVED:** Shopee widened Indonesia GMV-share leadership (46% → 54%). The Tokopedia-side of the market is reported as a **Combined** Tokopedia+TTS entity (~38% / ~US$21.9B), not as Legacy Tokopedia. TTS-labeled Indonesia GMV (~US$13.1B) and APJII TTS access (12.2% → 27.4%) rose sharply; Legacy Tokopedia access stayed near-flat (~9.4% → 9.6%). Momentum Works publicly links Indonesia’s slow GMV growth partly to **Tokopedia GMV rationalisation** after the TikTok acquisition.

**INFERRED (labeled):** On the anti-Shopee / “Tokopedia-side” story, the best-supported reading is **TikTok-led commerce growth under a Combined reporting structure**, with Tokopedia functioning as the **local licensed marketplace / infrastructure partner** — **not** a demonstrated Legacy Tokopedia comeback.

**Not claimed:** Exact GMV split of Combined ~US$21.9B between Legacy and TTS (UNKNOWN). That Combined 38% means Tokopedia “came back” (INVALID comparison).

# 3. 2025 structural break

**Why 2025 is not directly comparable to Legacy Tokopedia 2024:**

1. **PRIMARY (GoTo PR, 31 Jan 2024):** Tokopedia and TTS Indonesia businesses combined under PT Tokopedia; TikTok controlling stake; GoTo minority + service fee.  
2. **PRIMARY (TikTok Newsroom):** Preceded by Oct 2023 TTS transaction pause after Mot Reg 31/2023 social-commerce restrictions; Dec 2023 partnership announcement.  
3. **OBSERVED (SECONDARY MW):** 2025 Indonesia share publishes **Combined** Tokopedia+TTS at ~38% / ~US$21.9B — **NOT COMPARABLE** to Legacy Tokopedia 23% (2024).  
4. **OBSERVED (SECONDARY_HIGH):** GoTo fee is calculated on **combined** Tokopedia+TTS GMV — reinforces combined commercial base.

**Business-model change (OBSERVED structure):** Content/discovery commerce (TikTok) + marketplace vehicle (Tokopedia) under TikTok control; Jun 2025 integrated Seller Center (Tokopedia and/or TTS).

# 5. Competitive movement

### What changed (OBSERVED / DERIVED)

- Shopee GMV share **+8 pp**; absolute GMV estimate up ~US$5.2B.  
- Shopee and TTS **access** shares rose sharply (APJII).  
- Market reporting for Tokopedia-side switched from **split** to **Combined**.  
- Combined vs additive 2024 baseline: **+4 pp** / +~$2.7B (DERIVED baseline + OBSERVED 2025).  
- TTS-labeled Indonesia GMV large (~$13.1B).  
- Indonesia total GMV barely grew.

### What did not change / is not evidenced

- No proven Legacy Tokopedia standalone GMV rebound.  
- Legacy Tokopedia access did **not** surge.  
- No valid claim that “Tokopedia rose from 23% to 38%.”  
- Combined − TTS residual **not** used as observed Legacy GMV.

2. **#2 in MW-style tables** is **Combined Tokopedia+TTS**, not Legacy Tokopedia.

# 9. Implications (no overstated causality)

**Strategic implication (INFERRED, labeled):**

- Treating “Tokopedia” as if it regained 2024-style standalone strength misreads the structural break.  
- Competitive monitoring should track **three** Tokopedia-side concepts (Legacy / TTS / Combined), not one.  
- Shopee’s 2025 position strengthened on published GMV-share estimates even as a Combined challenger sits at ~38%.  
- Any narrative that “Tokopedia came back because of TikTok” must separate **Combined presence** from **Legacy recovery** — only the former is evidenced; the latter is not.

**Not claimed:** That TikTok “caused” Shopee’s share gain, or that rationalisation alone “caused” Indonesia’s slow GMV growth — those would be causal overreach. The evidence supports **association and structure**, not a full causal model.
