# PART 1 — 2022–2024 Competitive Baseline

**Source family:** Momentum Works Indonesia platform GMV-share estimates via secondary press (MEDIUM). Access: APJII (access ≠ GMV).

### Trajectory (OBSERVED shares)

| Year | Shopee | Legacy Tokopedia | TTS (standalone) | Indonesia total GMV |
|------|-------:|-----------------:|-----------------:|--------------------:|
| 2022 | 36% | 35% | ~5% (LOW confidence citation) | — |
| 2023 | 40% | 30% | 9% | US$53.8B |
| 2024 | 46% (~US$26B) | 23% | 11% | US$56.5B |

**DERIVED gap (Shopee − Tokopedia share):** 2022: +1 pp · 2023: +10 pp · 2024: +23 pp.

### What the historical evidence says

1. **Shopee’s position strengthened** (OBSERVED): share rose 36% → 40% → 46% (2022–2024).  
2. **Legacy Tokopedia’s published share declined** (OBSERVED): 35% → 30% → 23%.  
3. **TTS emerged as a third force** (OBSERVED): ~5–9–11% over 2022–2024 (2022 figure LOW confidence).  
4. **Pre-break landscape (end-2024):** Shopee clear #1; Tokopedia still #2 standalone but materially weaker vs 2022; TTS already double-digit; additive Tokopedia+TTS ≈ 34% (DERIVED) — already a potential #2 bloc if combined.

### Inflection points (CONTEXT / OBSERVED events)

- Late 2023 Mot Reg 31/2023 + TTS transaction pause (PRIMARY).  
- Dec 2023 / Jan 2024 TikTok–GoTo combination announced/completed (PRIMARY).  
- These sit **after** the 2022–2023 share gap had already widened — association, not proven sole cause of Shopee’s 2022–2024 rise.

## Gate 6 conclusion (one paragraph)

From 2022–2024, Shopee strengthened while Legacy Tokopedia’s published share fell. The Tokopedia–TTS combination addressed a **regulatory/operating problem** for TikTok Shop and pooled marketplace + content capabilities under TikTok control. In 2025, comparing **Shopee (54%) vs Combined (38%)** shows a real #2 challenger that still trails — **not** a Legacy Tokopedia comeback. Forward scenarios should track the Combined ecosystem vs Shopee while keeping Legacy 2025 GMV **UNKNOWN**.
