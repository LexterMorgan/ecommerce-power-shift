## Cross-hypothesis decision rule used

1. Prefer OBSERVED entity-labeled metrics over Combined misreads.  
2. Reject residual Combined − TTS as observed Legacy GMV.  
3. Reject APJII → GMV conversion.  
4. Support H2 for structure only unless both sides show material independent growth evidence.  
5. Rank H3 above H2 when TTS-labeled scale + access + control + rationalisation align and Legacy indicators do not.

## Bottom line

**Who drove the shift?** Shopee’s published share expansion is the clearest market-level movement. On the Tokopedia side of the historical dyad, evidence best supports **TikTok-led growth within a Combined entity (H3)**, with hybrid structure (H2 partial) and **no supported Legacy comeback (H1 unsupported)**.
