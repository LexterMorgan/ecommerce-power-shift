## How to read hypothesis columns

- **H1 (Tokopedia comeback)** needs evidence that **Legacy Tokopedia** strengthened on its own. Combined 38% does **not** count. Residual decline (if trusted) and “rationalisation” language **contradict** H1. Result so far: **weak / unsupported**.
- **H2 (Hybrid recovery)** needs evidence the combination itself created joint strength. Seller Center + official “choose Tokopedia and/or TTS” + modest combined share rise 34→38 **partially** support capability/hybrid positioning, but Shopee still gained more share. Result: **partial**.
- **H3 (TikTok-led growth)** needs evidence TTS drove the combined outcome. TTS Indonesia GMV ~13.1B with ~111% derived growth, TikTok control, and MW rationalisation language around Tokopedia **currently provide the strongest support**, with residual math as a **low-confidence** corroboration only.

## Mandatory non-claim

Do **not** write: “Tokopedia increased from 23% (2024) to 38% (2025).”  
Correct: “Legacy Tokopedia was ~23% in 2024 (secondary estimate); the **combined** Tokopedia + TikTok Shop entity was ~38% in 2025 (secondary estimate).”
