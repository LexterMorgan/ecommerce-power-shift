### Evidence FOR H1

- Seller Center permits selling on Tokopedia only (PRIMARY capability).  
- Tiny APJII access uptick (+0.17 pp) — **not** material recovery.  
- GoTo associate accounting shows Tokopedia entity profitability in 2025 disclosures (SECONDARY) — **not** Legacy marketplace GMV share.

### Evidence AGAINST H1

- No standalone 2025 GMV/share.  
- APJII Tokopedia access essentially flat while TTS and Shopee surged.  
- MW PRIMARY qualitative: Tokopedia GMV **rationalisation** contributed to Indonesia’s slow GMV growth.  
- Combined 38% cannot be credited to Legacy Tokopedia.

### Missing evidence

Standalone Legacy Tokopedia 2025 GMV, share, national sellers, Trends, traffic.

### H1 status

**UNSUPPORTED**

(If forced to speak only about GMV/share availability: the recovery claim is also **UNDETERMINED** on GMV — but available non-GMV and qualitative evidence do **not** support a material Legacy comeback, so the exploratory verdict is **UNSUPPORTED**, not “maybe yes.”)

## H2 — Hybrid recovery test

**Claim:** “Recovery is genuinely hybrid, with both TikTok and Tokopedia **materially** contributing.”

### Evidence FOR H2

- Legal combination under PT Tokopedia (PRIMARY).  
- Integrated Seller Center; sell on Tokopedia and/or TTS (PRIMARY).  
- Combined share vs additive 2024 baseline: 34% → 38% (DERIVED + OBSERVED) — Combined entity exists and is sizeable.  
- GoTo fee on combined GMV (SECONDARY_HIGH).

### Evidence AGAINST treating H2 as “both recovered”

- “Tokopedia is in the Combined entity” ≠ “Tokopedia recovered.”  
- No material Legacy access rebound.  
- No standalone Legacy GMV recovery proof.  
- MW rationalisation language implies Legacy GMV cleanup/shrink pressure, not boom.  
- Shopee gained more share (+8 pp) than Combined vs additive baseline (+4 pp).

### H2 status

**PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** for **hybrid operating structure**; **UNSUPPORTED** as proof that Legacy Tokopedia **materially** drove 2025 GMV growth.
